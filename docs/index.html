Installation

The installer includes matching firmware and a native flasher. No Rust, Node, ARM GNU, Arduino, or Homebrew installation is needed.

macOS 12+

  1. Choose the Apple Silicon or Intel DMG. Check your chip in Apple menu → About This Mac.
  2. Open the DMG and drag Teensy Panel into Applications.
  3. Open the app from Applications.

Builds are not Apple-notarized. You may need to approve the app in System Settings → Privacy & Security (System Preferences on macOS 12).

Windows 10 / 11 · x64

  1. Download the 64-bit Setup EXE.
  2. Run the installer and follow the prompts.
  3. Open Teensy Panel. WebView2 is included for offline installation.

Installers are not Authenticode-signed, so SmartScreen may appear. App-update signatures are separate from platform signatures.

Teensy 4.1 firmware

Desktop updates do not automatically flash hardware. Install the bundled firmware separately from inside the app.

Stop experiments and save your data before flashing. Flashing resets temporary board settings and data.

  1. Connect only the Teensy 4.1 you intend to flash. Close other serial tools and Teensy loaders.
  2. Open App & firmware, acknowledge the preparation steps, and click Install bundled firmware.
  3. Press the Teensy Program button when prompted. Keep USB connected until flashing completes.
  4. Wait for the board to restart, then click Refresh and Connect Teensy.
